2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide the application's account, civic complaint, location, notification, and related Panchayat services.

The categories of information we may collect include the following.

2.1 Account and Identity Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Authentication credentials and authentication-related information
  • Account identifiers associated with your account

Your password is used for authentication and is not intended to be displayed to other users.

2.2 Civic Complaint Information

When you submit a complaint or civic service request, we may collect:

  • Complaint category
  • Complaint description
  • Images or photographs that you voluntarily upload
  • Location associated with the complaint
  • Latitude and longitude
  • Additional information that you voluntarily provide as part of the complaint

This information is used to allow Panchayat personnel to understand, locate, process, and resolve reported civic issues.

2.3 Location Information

The application may request access to your device's location when you choose to use location-based functionality.

Location information may include:

  • Latitude
  • Longitude
  • Location associated with a reported civic issue

Location is used to:

  • Associate a civic complaint with its reported location.
  • Display the reported location on a map.
  • Help authorized Panchayat personnel identify and respond to reported issues.

The application does not require continuous background location tracking for ordinary complaint submission.

We request location access only when it is required for the relevant location-based feature.

2.4 Photos and Images

If you choose to upload an image while submitting a complaint or using another supported feature, we collect and store that image.

Images may be used by authorized Panchayat personnel to:

  • Understand the reported problem.
  • Verify the reported condition.
  • Process the complaint.
  • Monitor resolution of the complaint.

Images uploaded as part of a civic complaint may be accessible to authorized personnel who need access to the complaint to perform their official duties.

2.5 Notification Information

If you enable notifications, the application may process information necessary to deliver push notifications to your device.

Notifications may be used for:

  • Complaint status updates
  • Account-related notifications
  • Service-related notifications
  • Panchayat announcements
  • Other relevant application notifications

2.6 Technical Information

The application and its supporting services may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

Depending on the platform and services used, this may include information such as:

  • Device/platform information
  • Application version
  • Network-related information
  • Technical error or diagnostic information
  • Notification/device identifiers required for push notification delivery

We do not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary for the application's functionality.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain necessary Panchayat records, protect security, resolve complaints, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Different types of information may therefore have different retention periods.

Account Information

Account information is retained while the account remains active or as necessary to provide the service.

When an account deletion request is completed, personal account information is deleted or anonymized as described in the Account Deletion section below.

Civic Complaint Records

Civic complaints may need to remain in Panchayat operational records after an account deletion request because they may be necessary for:

  • Maintaining historical civic records.
  • Tracking work performed.
  • Maintaining administrative records.
  • Preventing loss of operational information.
  • Complying with applicable record-keeping requirements.

Where continued retention of a complaint is necessary, personal identifiers associated with the deleted account are removed or anonymized to prevent the complaint from being linked to the former user's identity.

We do not retain personal information indefinitely merely for convenience.

9. Account Deletion

Gorantla Grama Panchayati provides users with the ability to request deletion of their account.

Users can initiate account deletion:

  • Within the application: Profile → Delete Account
  • Externally: https://api.grampanchayat.digital/delete-account

When an account deletion request is successfully processed, we delete or anonymize the personal information associated with the account as applicable.

This may include:

  • Password/authentication information
  • Authentication tokens
  • Notification tokens associated with the account
  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Other personal account information that is no longer required

Civic Complaint Records

Civic complaint records may be retained when necessary for legitimate operational, administrative, legal, or record-keeping purposes.

When such records must be retained, personally identifying account information is anonymized so that the retained complaint cannot reasonably be associated with the deleted user's identity.

For example, an account may be represented as:
"Deleted Citizen"
and identifying contact information may be replaced with non-identifying values.

The purpose of this retention is to preserve legitimate Panchayat operational records without continuing to retain the former user's identifiable account information.

Account deletion is not merely account suspension or deactivation.

10. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information through the in-app deletion option or the external deletion webpage.

Deletion requests will be processed in accordance with our account deletion procedures.

Certain information may be retained or anonymized when retention is reasonably necessary for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • Dispute resolution
  • Legitimate Panchayat operational records
  • Other legally permitted record-keeping requirements

Where information is retained for these purposes, we limit retention to what is reasonably necessary.
