Gorantla Grama Panchayati Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Welcome to the Gorantla Grama Panchayati mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience while using our civic services app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we may collect the following types of information when you register and use the app:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and account authentication information.
  • Civic Requests: Complaint/service-request information, text descriptions of issues, and images uploaded by users.
  • Location Data: Latitude and longitude information collected when reporting a civic issue to accurately place the complaint on the map.
  • Technical Data: Device push notification tokens and necessary technical information required for app functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly to provide and improve civic services, including:

  • Creating and managing your user account.
  • Processing and resolving your submitted civic complaints.
  • Contacting you regarding your tickets or providing official Panchayat announcements.
  • Sending email delivery verifications and push notifications.

3. Location Information

The app uses location services to allow you to report the precise geographical coordinates of civic issues. This location is displayed on Google Maps within the app so authorized Panchayat officials can locate and resolve the issue. We do not track your location in the background when the app is closed.

4. Photos and Uploaded Content

Uploaded complaint images are securely stored on our Virtual Private Server (VPS) and may be accessed by authorized Panchayat officials and employees for the purpose of validating and processing complaints.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is only shared with authorized Panchayat officials and staff members responsible for resolving your complaints. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our VPS-hosted PostgreSQL database. We use industry-standard security measures, including authentication tokens and secure connections, to protect your information against unauthorized access or alteration.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information as long as your account is active. Civic complaint records and uploaded images may be retained indefinitely as part of the official Panchayat historical and operational records.

8. Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account at any time. You can do this directly within the mobile app by navigating to your Profile settings, or by visiting our Account Deletion Request page. When an account is deleted, your personal profile data is securely anonymized or destroyed. Civic complaints you have submitted will be retained for historical record-keeping, but will be anonymized and disassociated from your personal identity.

9. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adult residents of the Panchayat. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

10. Third-Party Services

We utilize the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Google Maps: Used to display issue locations.
  • Gmail SMTP / Nodemailer: Used for delivering verification and password reset emails.
  • Expo Push Notifications: Used to send updates about your tickets.

11. User Rights

You may access, update, or correct your personal information directly within the app's profile section.

12. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be available within the app, and significant changes may be communicated via in-app notifications.

13. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the Gorantla Grama Panchayati office using the official contact information provided within the app or the physical Panchayat office.
